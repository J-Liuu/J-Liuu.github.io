p5.js Flower

An interactive 3D flower generated with trigonometric functions and parametric equations, inspired by János Karsai.

Primary role Interactive Developer
Project focus Procedural 3D, Mathematical Visualization

Mathematical approach

Jacky Liu
  1. 01
    Polar coordinate petals

    Used angular sampling and polar coordinates to generate the repeating petal formations.

  2. 02
    Power-controlled curvature

    Applied power functions to tune the sharpness and curvature of each petal.

  3. 03
    Exponential bloom profile

    Used exponential decay to form the gradual cone-like transition from the stem into the bloom.

  4. 04
    Bump mapping

    Added a procedural bump function to create organic texture variation along the petal edges.

Interactive generative art
